# Positioning and go-to-market

A short composition for positioning a brand or offer and planning market entry or launch. Methods: positioning and pricing power in [../references/brand-growth.md](../references/brand-growth.md); choice logic in [../references/strategic-choice.md](../references/strategic-choice.md); research in [../references/research.md](../references/research.md); measurement and pilots in [../references/action-measurement.md](../references/action-measurement.md); local conditions in [../markets/tanzania.md](../markets/tanzania.md).

## Steps

1. **Decision frame.** What is being positioned or launched, for which market scope, by when, with what success metric and constraints.
2. **Competitive alternatives as customers see them.** Include informal substitutes, doing nothing and adjacent categories. Evidence: behaviour, switching accounts, price and availability checks.
3. **Target and context.** People × situation where the offer can win; check reachability and economics. Use segment-level evidence rather than national averages.
4. **Value and proof.** The progress customers seek, the capability that delivers it, the proof. Apply the competitor-substitution test to the claim.
5. **Frame of reference.** The category or use context that makes the value obvious.
6. **Route to market.** Channels (formal and informal), partners, distribution economics, trade margins, payment methods, service requirements. Test whether a competitor's route transfers (transfer test).
7. **Pricing and pack.** Price points by channel, affordability evidence for the target segment, margin logic, willingness-to-pay evidence and its strength.
8. **Alternatives.** At least two credible GTM routes (e.g. concentrated city launch vs distributor-led regional roll-out; direct vs partner-led) plus doing nothing or delaying.
9. **Launch sequence and learning plan.** Reversible first steps, pilot design with controls, leading indicators, decision rules for scale-up.
10. **Verify** with the gates most likely to fail here: customer relevance, competitive response, brand credibility, commercial mechanism, local validity, feasibility.
11. **Deliver** a positioning statement with proof and trade-offs, and a GTM plan with sequence, economics ranges, risks and the learning plan.

## Watch for

- Positioning for everyone.
- Rejecting a route (app, e-commerce, premium, modern trade) solely because of national aggregate statistics.
- Treating stated interest or synthetic respondents as proof of demand.
- Launch plans with no stop or scale criteria.
