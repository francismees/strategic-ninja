# Campaign and communications strategy

A short composition of shared modules for campaigns, launches and communications plans (campaign/quarter horizon, though diagnosis may need depth). Communications chain and brief: [../references/action-measurement.md](../references/action-measurement.md). Diagnosis: [diagnose.md](diagnose.md). Insight: [../references/insight.md](../references/insight.md). Measurement: [../references/action-measurement.md](../references/action-measurement.md). Template: [../templates/creative-brief.md](../templates/creative-brief.md).

## Steps

1. **Confirm communications is the right lever.** Restate the business objective and the behaviour that must change. Check quickly whether the constraint is availability, price, product or execution rather than communication; if so, say so and redirect before briefing creative. A request for "a viral campaign" is reframed into a business outcome, a behaviour and a communications task.
2. **Audience and occasion.** Define who (by behaviour and context, with evidence) and when. Avoid demographic caricatures; treat labels like "Gen Z" as descriptors to test, not explanations.
3. **Barrier or opportunity.** Identify, with evidence, why the behaviour is not happening. Label hypotheses.
4. **Communications task.** State what communication can do about the barrier (build memory links to buying situations, reframe value, reassure, remind, announce availability).
5. **Proposition and support.** One single-minded, true, motivating thought with proof; test generic claims with the competitor-substitution test.
6. **Brand assets and tone.** Use distinctive assets; validate language and cultural references locally (for Tanzania, see [../markets/tanzania.md](../markets/tanzania.md)).
7. **Channel roles.** Assign what each channel must do; check reach among the audience with evidence (subscriptions and followers are not people reached).
8. **Measurement before launch.** Exposure, behavioural response, brand effects and business outcome, each with baseline, source, cadence and decision rule; plan a comparison (holdout regions or outlets) when attribution matters.
9. **Verify.** Gates: problem framing, customer relevance, brand credibility, commercial mechanism, local validity, measurement, clarity. Anti-patterns: premature creative, exposure as impact, copied tactic.
10. **Deliver** a campaign strategy on a page and the creative brief. Creative ideas, if requested, come after the brief and are labelled as illustrations for the team to develop.

## Post-launch review

If reach is strong but behaviour does not change, do not declare success. Locate the failed mechanism (wrong audience, weak proposition, message not linked to the brand, product unavailable, price barrier) using the chain of measures, and decide continue/change/stop by the pre-agreed rule.
