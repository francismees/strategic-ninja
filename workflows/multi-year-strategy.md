# Multi-year strategy (two-to-three years and five-year direction)

A short composition for longer horizons. The longer the horizon, the less credible point forecasts become: build scenarios, signposts, capabilities and staged commitments. Methods: [../references/strategic-choice.md](../references/strategic-choice.md), [../references/brand-growth.md](../references/brand-growth.md), [../references/research.md](../references/research.md), [../references/analytics.md](../references/analytics.md) (trend and forecast cautions), [../references/action-measurement.md](../references/action-measurement.md), [../markets/tanzania.md](../markets/tanzania.md).

## Steps

1. **Frame the strategic question** and the horizon. Two-to-three years: which structural positions, capabilities and portfolio bets? Five years: what future position and optionality should be built?
2. **Structural drivers.** Identify forces likely to shape the category: demographics and urbanisation, income, technology and connectivity, regulation and tax, channel evolution, competition, input costs, climate and logistics. For each, record current evidence (dated, scoped) and the key uncertainty.
3. **Critical uncertainties.** Select the two or three drivers that are both highly uncertain and highly consequential for the decision.
4. **Scenarios.** Build three or four internally consistent scenarios from those uncertainties. Describe each in plain terms with indicative ranges, not precise forecasts. Do not assign spurious probabilities.
5. **Strategic position and capabilities.** For the preferred direction, define where to play, how to win, and the capabilities needed. Test each against every scenario.
6. **Classify moves.**
   - *No-regret moves*: sensible in all scenarios.
   - *Options*: small investments that preserve the right to scale if a scenario unfolds.
   - *Big bets*: commitments justified only in some scenarios; state the conditions.
7. **Signposts and triggers.** For each scenario, the observable indicators (with sources and cadence) that show it is unfolding, and the decision each trigger unlocks.
8. **Staging and economics.** Phase investments; show ranges and sensitivities; identify irreversible commitments and their timing.
9. **Verify.** Especially: evidence (dated structural data), logic (no single-point forecast carrying the argument), meaningful choice, feasibility, local validity, measurement (signposts are measurable).
10. **Deliver** a direction on a page, scenario summaries, move classification, signposts and the next 12-month actions.

## Handling a single precise forecast

If a plan rests on one exact growth figure over five years, replace it with a range across scenarios, show which decisions are sensitive to it, and identify the signposts that would tell leadership which range is materialising.
