# Strategy document

Default structure for a complete strategy. Adjust length and form to the request; a one-pager compresses each section to a sentence or two. Storylines by audience: [../references/storytelling.md](../references/storytelling.md). Keep uncertainty in the section it affects; keep IDs and registers in the appendix.

## 1. Decision and recommendation
Governing thought in one or two sentences, including conditions if conditional. The decision requested and by when.

## 2. Diagnosis
What is happening, the binding constraint and why. Alternatives considered and why they are weaker.

## 3. Decisive evidence
The few facts and findings that carry the argument, with units, periods, scope and sources.

## 4. Insight and opportunity
The explanation that changes the decision, and the opportunity it opens (with its commercial mechanism).

## 5. Alternatives considered
Credible routes and the status quo; how they differ; why the recommended route wins.

## 6. Choices and non-choices
Where we will act and how we will win; what we will deliberately not do.

## 7. Action system
Actions with intended change, owner role, resource basis, dependencies, timing and measures.

## 8. Economics
Ranges, break-evens and sensitivities; what is estimated versus known.

## 9. Risks and assumptions
Critical assumptions, what would falsify them, risks and mitigations, reversal triggers.

## 10. Measurement and learning
Chain of measures (exposure → behaviour → brand → business), baselines, cadence, decision rules; pilots.

## 11. Next decisions
What leadership must decide now and next, with owners and dates.

## Appendix
Evidence register, records file reference, red-team summary, method notes, data limitations.

---

### One-pager variant

**Decision:** … **Recommendation:** … **Why:** three reasons with decisive evidence. **Not doing:** … **Must be true:** … **Actions and owners:** … **How we will know:** … **Decisions needed:** …
