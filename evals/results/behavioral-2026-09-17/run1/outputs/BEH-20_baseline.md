**It's safe but not strong enough.** "Refreshingly" is a word every soda uses, and any local rival can call itself "Tanzanian," so the line shows where you're from but gives no reason to pick you over Coke, Pepsi or another local brand.

"Refreshingly X" can also suggest being Tanzanian is a pleasant surprise, and an English-only line limits mass-market reach. Link local pride to something only you own (a flavour, ingredient or moment), and test a Swahili version with your core buyers before you commit.
