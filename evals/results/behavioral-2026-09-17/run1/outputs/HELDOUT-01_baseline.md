## Why the headline changed

- **"Tanzania's" is wider than the data.** The audit covers three cities, and only modern trade and large dukas. It doesn't cover Dodoma, Mbeya or Tabora, or the small dukas and open markets where most upcountry rice sells.
- **"No.1" without "by value" doesn't hold.** By kilos you're third: 14%, against Shamba Gold's 31% and Pwani Pearl's 19%. Distributors think in bags and tonnes. Anyone who already carries Shamba Gold will know this, and if slide 2 falls apart, they'll doubt the rest of the deck too.
- **There's a legal and data-use angle.** Tanzania's Fair Competition Act covers misleading claims in trade, so a claim you can't back up is a risk as well as a credibility problem. Also check whether Kilele's terms let you quote its data outside the company.

**A line for the MD:** "We keep No.1 in the headline. We just say what we're No.1 in. Nobody can argue with 'by value,' but Shamba Gold's rep could shoot down 'Tanzania's No.1' using our own audit."
