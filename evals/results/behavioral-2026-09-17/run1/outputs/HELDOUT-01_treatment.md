## Why not "Tanzania's No.1"

1. **The audit doesn't cover their towns.** It only includes Dar, Arusha and Mwanza. Dodoma, Mbeya and Tabora aren't in it, and those are the markets these distributors know best.
2. **You're No.1 by value but No.3 by volume.** Shamba Gold has 31% of kilos, more than twice your 14%. Pwani Pearl has 19%. Distributors plan trucks and warehouse space in bags. An unqualified "No.1" will sound like "sells the most rice," and some of them may have sales records that show otherwise.
3. **It only covers one kind of shop.** The audit measures modern trade and large dukas. It leaves out small dukas, open markets and loose rice. My guess is those channels matter more upcountry, but the audit can't show that.

If slide 2 overclaims, the distributors will doubt every number that follows, including the margin and support figures you most need them to believe. Also, if this line ever goes into ads, packaging or trade materials, have legal check it against the rules on misleading claims first.

**Line for your MD:** "We keep No.1. We just say what we're No.1 in. The qualified version holds up when a distributor checks it against his own stock records. The national version doesn't."

## Questions to be ready for

- **"Who sells the most rice?"** Shamba Gold, by kilos. Your answer: they sell more kilos at a lower price, and we bring in more shillings. On average, a kilo of Mchele Bora is worth about 2.7 times a kilo of Shamba Gold (27/14 vs 22/31). Then move on to what a bag of Mchele Bora earns the distributor, using your actual trade terms.
- **"Does it sell here?"** The audit can't answer that, so don't suggest it does. Bring whatever upcountry evidence you have, such as existing orders or wholesaler pull, plus your launch support plan.
- **"Are you No.1 in every city?"** We don't know. The 27% combines all three cities. Check whether Kilele can give you a split by city before anyone makes that claim.
