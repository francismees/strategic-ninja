# Parent interviews on paying school fees (synthetic fixture)

> **Invented for evaluation.** Six in-depth interviews, Dodoma and Dar es Salaam, July 2026. Kiswahili interviews; English translations by the moderator (not independently checked). Recruitment: parents with at least one child in a private primary school.

**P1 (mother, Dodoma):** "Nilipeleka pesa benki mara ya mwisho, lakini sasa nalipa kwa simu. Ila shule inataka risiti ya benki." — "Last time I took the money to the bank, but now I pay by phone. But the school wants a bank receipt."
**P2 (father, Dar es Salaam):** "Simu ni rahisi. Ila makato..." — "Phone is easy. But the charges..." [trails off; moderator did not probe]
**P3 (mother, Dar es Salaam):** "Mimi nalipa kidogo kidogo kila mwezi kwa simu." — "I pay little by little every month by phone."
**P4 (father, Dodoma):** "Napendelea kulipa mwenyewe shuleni ili nione." — "I prefer to pay at the school myself so that I can see."
**P5 (mother, Dar es Salaam):** "Inategemea. Kama nina salio, simu. Kama sina, nasubiri." — "It depends. If I have balance, phone. If not, I wait."
**P6 (grandmother paying for grandchild, Dodoma):** "Mjukuu wangu ananisaidia kutuma." — "My grandchild helps me send it."
