# Cooler pilot economics (synthetic demonstration)

Inputs: [../data/cooler_pilot.csv](../data/cooler_pilot.csv) and items 5-6 of [../data/market_and_plan_notes.md](../data/market_and_plan_notes.md). All values fictional. Calculation performed with Python arithmetic during the build; reproduced here step by step.

1. **Counterfactual for cooler outlets** (pre-period level × growth in matched outlets): 40.2 × (42.2 ÷ 39.8) = 42.62 litres/week of 350 ml.
2. **Incremental volume**: 63.5 − 42.62 = **20.88 litres/week** per cooler outlet (**49%** above the counterfactual).
3. **Annual contribution**: 20.88 × 52 × TZS 700 = TZS 0.760 million; minus maintenance TZS 0.25 million = **TZS 0.510 million per year**.
4. **Payback** on TZS 1.6 million installed cost: 1.6 ÷ 0.510 = **3.1 years** (5-year life: about TZS 0.95 million net, undiscounted).
5. **Break-even for a two-year payback**: (1.6 ÷ 2 + 0.25) million ÷ (52 × 700) = **28.9 incremental litres/week**.
6. At a 49% uplift, that requires a pre-cooler baseline of about **59 litres/week** of 350 ml.

**Interpretation limits.** Ten weeks after installation in 22 Dar es Salaam outlets; matched rather than randomised controls; cooler visibility and chilling effects are not separated; margin and cost figures are internal estimates or quotes. The uplift may not hold in Mwanza or in outlets with different footfall.
