# Brand tracker H1 2026 (synthetic fixture)

Same survey as Demo A (urban adults 18-45 CSD buyers; base 612; Dar es Salaam, Mwanza, Arusha).

| Measure | H1 2025 | H1 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Prompted awareness | 91% | 90% |
| Consideration | 52% | 51% |
| Fizzi rated "good value" (agree) | 58% | 57% |
| Brand that comes to mind for "a cold drink on the way home" | not asked | not asked |

The tracker does not measure brand retrieval in specific buying situations.
