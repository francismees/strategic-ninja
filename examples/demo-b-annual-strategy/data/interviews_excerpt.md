# Commuter and duka shopper interviews (synthetic fixture)

> **Invented for the demonstration.** Respondents, quotations and translations are fictional. Ten short intercept interviews, May 2026, at two Dar es Salaam bus stages (R01-R06) and one Mwanza market street (R07-R10). Recruitment: adults who had bought any soft drink in the past week. Interviews in Kiswahili; English glosses by the analyst (not independently verified).

| ID | Context | Quote (original) | Analyst gloss |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | Dar, bus stage, 17:30, bought 350 ml Fizzi cold | "Nikishuka tu nataka kitu cha baridi." | "As soon as I get off [the bus] I want something cold." |
| R02 | Dar, bus stage, bought competitor 350 ml | "Fizzi ilikuwa ya moto, nikachukua hii." | "Fizzi was warm, so I took this one." |
| R03 | Dar, bus stage, bought nothing | "Hakuna friji hapa. Nitanunua nyumbani." | "There's no fridge here. I'll buy at home." |
| R04 | Dar, bus stage, bought 350 ml Fizzi | "Chupa ndogo inatosha njiani." | "A small bottle is enough on the way." |
| R05 | Dar, duka near stage, bought water | "Soda ya moto hainywi." | "A warm soda isn't drinkable." |
| R06 | Dar, bus stage, bought 500 ml competitor cold | "Nilitaka ndogo lakini ya baridi ilikuwa hii tu." | "I wanted a small one, but this was the only cold one." |
| R07 | Mwanza, market street, bought nothing | "Siku hizi Fizzi haipatikani sana hapa." | "These days Fizzi isn't easy to find here." |
| R08 | Mwanza, market street, bought competitor 350 ml | "Duka la kawaida halina Fizzi wiki hii." | "My usual duka hasn't had Fizzi this week." |
| R09 | Mwanza, market street, bought 1.25 L Fizzi for home | "Kwa familia nachukua kubwa, ni nafuu." | "For the family I take the big one, it's cheaper." |
| R10 | Mwanza, market street, bought 350 ml Fizzi cold | "Bei ni sawa, shida ni kupata ya baridi." | "The price is fine, the problem is finding a cold one." |

Analyst coding (sample counts, not population estimates):
- Cold availability decides the on-the-go purchase: R01, R02, R03, R05, R06, R10 (6 of 10).
- Fizzi hard to find (Mwanza): R07, R08 (2 of 4 Mwanza respondents).
- Price mentioned as a barrier: none; R10 explicitly says price is fine (1 of 10 mentions price as acceptable).
- Large pack chosen for family value: R09 (1 of 10).
