# Examples

> Everything in this folder is a **synthetic teaching fixture** unless it is explicitly marked as a real, verified source (only NBS CPI, TCRA and World Bank figures copied from `markets/tanzania-observations.json`). Company names, data, respondents and quotations are invented. Do not cite them as market evidence.

| Example | Use it to see |
|---|---|
| [insight-calibration.md](insight-calibration.md) | Four insight cases: an observation mislabelled as insight, an appealing but unsupported psychological story, an evidence-backed consumer mechanism, and an operational/commercial insight with no human tension |
| [calibration-cases.md](calibration-cases.md) | Ten short counterexamples and boundary cases that calibrate critique in both directions (when to object, when not to) |
| [demo-a-diagnosis/](demo-a-diagnosis/README.md) | **Workflow A** end to end: a solution-shaped "we need a new campaign" brief diagnosed as a distribution problem, with reconciled calculations and records |
| [demo-b-annual-strategy/](demo-b-annual-strategy/README.md) | **Workflow B** end to end: a 2027 annual plan from panel, pilot, qualitative, tracker, audit and market evidence, with options, economics, a validated storyline and a memory revision |
| [demo-c-critique/](demo-c-critique/README.md) | **Workflow C** end to end: red-teaming a flawed board deck, keeping its one sound action, repairing the storyline and showing what structural validation cannot catch |

The demos were written by the skill author following the workflows and running the scripts. They demonstrate intended reasoning and working traceability; they are not independent evidence that the skill improves real strategy work. Behavioural evaluations are in `evals/`.
