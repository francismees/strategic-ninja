# Radio weeks versus sign-ups: raw and stratified comparison (synthetic demonstration)

Input: [../data/weekly_signups_q2q3.csv](../data/weekly_signups_q2q3.csv) (26 weeks, fictional). Calculated with Python (mean weekly sign-ups by group) during the build.

| Comparison | Radio weeks | Non-radio weeks | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| All weeks (the deck's comparison) | 14,421 (n = 8) | 10,194 (n = 18) | 1.42 |
| Month-end salary weeks only | 16,829 (n = 5) | 15,511 (n = 1) | 1.09 |
| Other weeks only | 10,408 (n = 3) | 9,881 (n = 17) | 1.05 |

Five of the eight radio weeks were month-end salary weeks, against one of the eighteen non-radio weeks. Most of the raw 42% difference coincides with salary timing. Within strata the differences are 5-9% on very small numbers of weeks, which cannot establish a radio effect in either direction.

**Limits:** observational, 26 weeks, no geographic control, no adjustment for other activity. A causal estimate needs a designed test (for example regional holdouts with radio on and off in the same weeks).
