# Pesalo 2027 Growth Strategy (board pre-read, slide text)

> **Synthetic fixture.** Pesalo is a fictional mobile savings wallet. This deck was written to contain realistic strategic defects alongside some sound elements, for critique practice. Real TCRA figures are quoted on slide 2 exactly as a careless deck might misuse them.

**Slide 1 — Vision.** Pesalo will be Tanzania's number one Gen Z savings app by 2027.

**Slide 2 — A massive opportunity.** 117 million mobile users in Tanzania = 117 million potential Pesalo customers. (Source: TCRA, 2026.)

**Slide 3 — SWOT = our strategy.** Strengths: great app, young team. Weaknesses: small budget. Opportunities: Gen Z, digital boom. Threats: banks, M-wallet competitors. Strategy: leverage strengths to capture opportunities.

**Slide 4 — Consumer insight.** Gen Z are digital natives who crave instant gratification and hate traditional banks. They want savings to feel like a game.

**Slide 5 — Proof it works.** Kenyan youth adopted savings apps in record time (Nairobi Fintech Youth Report 2025). Tanzania will follow the same curve.

**Slide 6 — Positioning.** Pesalo: smart savings for smart people.

**Slide 7 — Premium growth.** Launch Pesalo Gold, a TZS 5,000 per month subscription with higher interest and rewards, targeting 100,000 subscribers in year one.

**Slide 8 — Campaign.** A viral #PesaloChallenge on TikTok with 20 influencers. Target: 10 million views and 1 million downloads.

**Slide 9 — Our marketing works.** In the Q2 radio test, weekly sign-ups were 40% higher in radio weeks. Radio ROI is proven.

**Slide 10 — Fix cash-out reliability.** 18% of cash-out attempts failed in Q2 because agents ran out of float. Sign a float-rebalancing partner covering our top 2,000 agents.

**Slide 11 — KPIs.** Brand awareness 60%; 500,000 TikTok followers; 1 million downloads.

**Slide 12 — The ask.** Approve TZS 2.5 billion for the Gen Z campaign and Pesalo Gold launch.

**Appendix A — Operating data (Q2 2026).** Registered accounts 1,400,000. Monthly active users (at least one transaction in 30 days) 210,000. 30-day retention of users who registered in Q2: 22%. Cash-out attempts 2.6 million; failed 468,000 (18%).
