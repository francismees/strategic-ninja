**Governing thought:** Pesalo loses most new users within a month, and nearly one in five cash-outs fails. Fix reliability and prove that link to churn before paying to acquire more users.

**Decision requested:** approve the reliability and onboarding work and a three-week cohort analysis now. Hold the TZS 2.5 billion acquisition and Gold spend until retention evidence meets an agreed threshold.

---

**1. Most new Pesalo users stop transacting within a month**
*Exhibit:* registered accounts (1.4m) → 30-day active (210,000, 15%) → 30-day retention of Q2 registrants (22%).
*Uncertainty:* retention definition not documented; only 30-day data available.

**2. Nearly one in five cash-out attempts failed in Q2, and failed withdrawals are the top complaint in one-star reviews**
*Exhibit:* 468,000 of 2.6m cash-outs failed (18%); 41 of 120 sampled one-star reviews mention failed withdrawals.
*Uncertainty:* reviewers are self-selected unhappy users; failure causes beyond agent float are not broken out.

**3. The growth problem looks like a leaking bucket, not a shortage of sign-ups; we will confirm the churn link within three weeks**
*Exhibit:* leaking-bucket diagram. The test: 30- and 90-day retention for users whose first cash-out failed versus succeeded.
*Uncertainty:* the link between failed cash-outs and churn is a hypothesis until the cohort analysis is done.

**4. The market is not 117 million customers: subscriptions exceed the population, so they are not people**
*Exhibit:* TCRA end-June 2026 telecom subscriptions (116,952,295; SIMs active in 90 days, including machine-to-machine) next to the World Bank 2025 population estimate (70,545,865, all ages).
*Uncertainty:* a people-based market size (adults with mobile money access in target areas) has not yet been built.

**5. The radio test does not prove ROI: most radio weeks were salary weeks**
*Exhibit:* raw comparison (+42%) next to salary-week and other-week comparisons (+9% and +5%, few weeks).
*Uncertainty:* the strata rest on very few weeks; only a regional holdout test can show whether radio works.

**6. Fund reliability and onboarding now; release acquisition spend only when retention clears an agreed threshold**
*Exhibit:* choice on a page. Where: top 2,000 agents and new users' first 30 days. How: a reliable first save-and-withdraw. Not doing: viral influencer campaign in H1 2027, Pesalo Gold before a priced test, national radio before a holdout test.
*Uncertainty:* conditional on the cohort analysis showing that failed cash-outs drive churn. If not, re-diagnose (fees, crashes, reasons for signing up).

**7. The viral campaign and Pesalo Gold are held, not killed: each needs evidence first**
*Exhibit:* options table with the evidence gap for each.
*Uncertainty:* willingness to pay for Gold is untested rather than disproven.
